Task 2 Reflection

In this homework my agent needed to make a decision and explain the evidence for it . If it said a company had a good sales team, I 
could go back to the customer interview and see if that explanation was valid. It made me think about how easily I accept an AI 
answer if it sounds confident or aligns with my existing beliefs.

AI sycophancy is when the model agrees with the user too readily. For instance, if I say, "My argument is strong, right?" the model 
might say, "That's a good argument" and overlook the weakest part. I’d be more likely to benefit from asking, “What part of my 
argument needs more evidence?” That gives me something I can check on and improve.

This kind of promise can be more serious in conversations when someone is talking about their fears. Sometimes people use the term 
“AI psychosis” to describe how conversations with chatbots can reinforce delusional beliefs. If someone says to the chatbot that 
they’re scared that strangers are watching them, the chatbot can validate that sounds scary, but it can also be explicit that it 
can’t confirm what’s happening. It can also encourage that person to talk with someone they trust or seek professional support. By 
saying the same thing over and over again the fear might begin to feel more real.

My take away is to ask what is an AI answer based on, and what is uncertain. In the Fisher agent I could check the numbers or the 
interview. In everyday use I can search for a trusted source, ask the model to question my assumptions or talk to a human when the 
situation requires it.
